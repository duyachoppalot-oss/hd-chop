# Data rights — what may be copied into hdchop

Not legal advice. Get a quick review before App Store launch.

| Source | May copy | Must NOT copy |
|---|---|---|
| OEM fitment extracted from HD catalogs (`hd_oem_catalog_reference`, `oem_fitment`) | Normalized OEM number, year, model code, a cleaned short description written by us, condition tags | Catalog text verbatim beyond a short part name, diagrams, images, page scans |
| HD parts catalogs scraped from the H-D service portal (serviceinfo.harley-davidson.com) into `~/Developer/catalog-extract/rows/*.tsv`, 391 books, 1991–2027 — loaded by `scripts/etl/09_service_catalog.mjs` (`fitment.source = 'service_catalog'`) | Same as the row above: normalized OEM number, year, model code, a cleaned short description written by us, condition tags. Section/figure names only internally (concept matching), never displayed | Catalog text verbatim beyond a short part name, section/figure titles shown as-is, diagrams and images (`catalog-extract/images`), page scans, the numeric part index as a list |
| ⚠ Open question for that source (Laken, before launch) | The portal is public, no login (the scraper never logs in). Its Terms of Use (serviceinfo.harley-davidson.com/terms, dated May 12, 2016, read 2026-10-24) say: "You may not modify, copy, reproduce, republish, repurpose, … sell or distribute … the material on the Site in whole or in part"; printing/downloading only "solely for your own use"; it also prohibits "harvesting, mining or collection of … information from or through the Site". No robots.txt. Whether bare fitment facts (number ↔ model ↔ year), re-expressed in our own words, count as "the material" is a legal question — get the pre-launch review to answer it. Laken accepted the risk for now and the source was applied 2026-10-24 (657,791 fitment rows, 20,913 parts). It stays removable (SQL in `packages/db/migrations/003_part_source.sql`): every fitment row and part it adds carries `source = 'service_catalog'` | — |
| H-D parts-catalog PDFs Laken holds (local, ~/Desktop/Stanky/parts-catalogs, ~/Downloads, ~/Downloads/hd-drive), OCR'd by `scripts/catalog` and loaded by `scripts/etl/11_catalog_books.mjs --apply` (`fitment.source = 'catalog_ocr'`, `oem_parts.source = 'catalog_ocr'`) | Same as the H-D catalog rows above: normalized OEM number, year, model code, a cleaned short part name written by us (first words of the OCR'd name), condition tags (74/80 ci). Model-years and specs from H-D model tables/spec pages (`vehicles`, `vehicle_specs`) as facts with the document + page cited | Page scans, OCR text, diagrams, notes or tables reproduced verbatim. The OCR cache stays outside the repo |

Removable as one source | Descriptions or tables reproduced verbatim; anything shown in the app as coming from the site |
| Our derived data (`oem_supersession`, `oem_part_timeline`, chain roots, concepts) | All of it | — |
